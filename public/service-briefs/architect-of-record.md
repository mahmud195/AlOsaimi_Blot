# Architect of Record

## What this service covers

Architect-of-record support helps move projects from design intent to authority-ready and permit-ready execution through documentation, municipal alignment, and code-aware follow-up.

## Who it is for

- Projects that need approvals and permit coordination
- Clients that need help meeting municipal requirements and Saudi Building Code expectations

## Key deliverables

- Permit documentation
- Municipal compliance review
- Authority coordination
- Code-aware design support

## Geography

The public site positions this service for projects in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
