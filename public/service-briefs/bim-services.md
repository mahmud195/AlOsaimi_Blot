# BIM Services

## What this service covers

BIM services at Al Osaimi Consulting focus on digital coordination, clash detection, quantity takeoffs, and model-based project understanding before construction begins.

## Who it is for

- Design teams
- Construction teams
- Operations stakeholders who need clearer coordination and better pre-construction visibility

## Key deliverables

- 3D models
- Clash detection
- Quantity takeoffs
- LOD-based deliverables

## Geography

The public site positions this service for projects in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
