# Design Services

## What this service covers

Design services combine creativity with buildability across architecture, interiors, urban design, landscape, structural systems, and electromechanical planning.

## Who it is for

- Clients that need concept development and coordinated design thinking
- Projects that require a balance of aesthetics, function, safety, and technical performance

## Key deliverables

- Architectural design
- Interior design
- Urban and landscape design
- Structural and electromechanical coordination

## Geography

The public site positions this service for projects in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
