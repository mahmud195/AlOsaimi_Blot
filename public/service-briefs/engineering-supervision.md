# Engineering Supervision

## What this service covers

Engineering supervision focuses on project planning follow-up, contractor coordination, bills of quantities, technical support, and issue resolution during implementation.

## Who it is for

- Owners and project teams that need quality-led execution support
- Projects with multiple stakeholders and technical coordination needs

## Key deliverables

- Technical reviews
- Execution monitoring
- Contractor coordination
- Quality observations and follow-up

## Geography

The public site positions this service for projects in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
