# Al Osaimi Consulting

## Company summary

Al Osaimi Consulting is a Saudi engineering consultancy focused on architecture, engineering, project management, supervision, surveying, architect-of-record support, and BIM delivery for built-environment projects.

## What the company does

- Provides project management for schedule, budget, scope, and delivery coordination
- Provides engineering supervision for execution follow-up, technical support, and quality oversight
- Provides integrated design services across architecture, interiors, landscape, structure, and electromechanical systems
- Provides surveying works for accurate site data and infrastructure support
- Provides architect-of-record services for permit-ready documentation and authority coordination
- Provides integrated engineering services for structural, MEP, and infrastructure delivery
- Provides BIM services for digital coordination, clash detection, quantity takeoffs, and model-based delivery

## Who it serves

The site positions the company as a fit for developers, owners, institutions, contractors, and project teams that need technically rigorous, coordinated, and delivery-oriented consultancy.
